**Cops & Criminals** is the club's law-enforcement system for Motor Town.
There is no game mod involved — the whole loop runs inside the club's
backend and talks to the game server directly.

## The core loop

Hauling **illicit cargo** (money pallets, moonshine, cocaine, ganja and
friends) builds your **criminal score**. Every delivery payment goes onto
the score, 100% of it.

- Deliveries can trigger a random **Wanted** status — the bigger the haul
  relative to your score, the higher the chance.
- Once wanted, **police** can chase you, pull you over, and **arrest** you.
- An arrest means **jail** and **confiscation** of your bounty — and the
  bounty comes off your score too.
- Escape and your wanted status decays; pull it off while cops are on duty
  and you even earn an **evasion bonus**.
