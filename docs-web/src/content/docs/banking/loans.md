## Summary

The Bank of ASEAN offers loans to verified citizens. Loan amounts are determined by driver experience, earnings history, and social standing. Interest is charged as a one-time fee at origination.

## 1. Eligibility

To take out a loan, you must:

- Be Discord-verified (linked account)

- Have a positive social score (negative scores make you ineligible)

## 2. Maximum Loan Calculation

Your maximum loan is determined by a series of factors applied in order:

_Loan Limit Factors (applied sequentially)_

| Step | Factor | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Base loan | $10,000 + (driver_level × $3,000) + (truck_level × $3,000) |
| 2 | Social score modifier | Multiplied by (1 + social_score × 0.05). Negative scores reduce, positive increase. |
| 3 | Bank policy cap | Hard cap at $6,000,000 |
| 4 | Earnings history cap | Capped at 5× your total lifetime delivery earnings |


## 3. Fee Structure

Loans carry a one-time origination fee calculated using a tiered system:

_Loan Fee Tiers_

| Tier | Portion of Loan | Interest Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | First 50% of max loan | 10% |
| Tier 2 | Next 25% of max loan | 20% |
| Tier 3 | Next 12.5% of max loan | 30% |
| Beyond | Remaining | 30% |

## 4. Repayment

Loans can be repaid through:

- Manual repayment — using the bank command in-game

- Auto-repayment — a portion of UBI and delivery profits is automatically applied

- Vehicle sale proceeds — 50% of vehicle sale value is applied to loans
